Walmart Return Policy Overview:
Standard Return Window: You have 90 days after purchase or receipt to return most items unless noted in specific category exceptions.

Category Exceptions:
- Consumer Electronics: Returnable within 30 days of receipt (e.g., TVs, laptops, cameras).
- Wireless Phones & Tablets: Returnable within 14 days of receipt.
- Fresh Produce & Grocery: Covered by the Fresh Guarantee—refundable with receipt in-store or online without needing to bring back the physical item.

Non-Returnable Items:
- Firearms, ammunition, air mattresses (opened), prescription medications, digital downloads, and Walmart gift cards.

Marketplace Sellers:
- Items purchased from third-party Marketplace sellers on Walmart.com generally have a 30-day return window.

Refund Methods & Restocking Fees:
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method (credit card: 5–7 business days; gift card: immediate).
- Walmart does not charge restocking fees for items sold directly by Walmart.
